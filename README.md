A top-down stealth heist game built for the [make.fun best-heist-game bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/best-heist-game-20261122-0559).

One quiet night, the gallery is empty — except for the guards, the cameras,
and the lasers. Slip through three rooms (Lobby, Painting Gallery, Gem
Vault), steal the **Emerald Moon** from its podium, and escape through the
emergency exit before the alarm hits 100.

- **Guards** patrol waypoints with sweeping vision cones; get spotted and the
  alarm climbs fast, guards give chase.
- **Cameras** sweep their cones; one EMP charge shuts them down for 6 seconds.
- **Lasers** pulse across corridors — time your crossing.
- **Tools at a cost**: 3 diversion coins (lure a guard), 1 EMP, 2 smoke bombs.
- **Multiple routes** per room, cover (statues, sofas, plants) that blocks
  sight, loot to grab on the way, and a final score screen with your rank.

All art is drawn procedurally on canvas and all sounds are synthesised with
WebAudio — no external assets, no network. Play with WASD/arrows, Shift to
sneak, 1/2/3 for tools, M to mute.
